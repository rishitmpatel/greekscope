About

greekscope prices European options with the Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) model. This page is a dependency-free port of the maths in the greekscope Python library. It is an educational tool, not trading advice.

Model assumptions

  • The underlying follows geometric Brownian motion with constant volatility.
  • The risk-free rate and continuous dividend yield are constant.
  • Frictionless markets: continuous trading, no transaction costs, no arbitrage.
  • Exercise only at expiry (European style); no early exercise or discrete dividends.

Real markets show volatility smiles, jumps and stochastic volatility, so implied vol varies by strike and expiry and model Greeks are approximations.
